# IELTS is EZ

IELTS is EZ is a free IELTS preparation website for learners who want a clear route through the exam. It keeps the existing lesson library for all five study areas, interactive trainers, 30 listening tests, 40 reading tests, 60 real writing tasks, timed practice, progress saved in the browser, and AI feedback where configured.

The product should always answer three learner questions quickly: what should I study, where did I leave off, and what should I practise next. The homepage explains the journey. The inner study area is a calm workspace for doing the work.

Accounts, paid plans, and a final public domain are future decisions. The interface must not imply that they already exist. This redesign is a local demo until Alex approves it for production.
