How these frames were made (4 October 2026)
- Drawn by Codex (CLI 0.155.1, built-in image generation, five runs). Codex could not produce real transparency (it painted a checkerboard), so every frame was drawn on flat magenta and the magenta was keyed out afterwards (alpha from min(R, B) - G, edge pixels un-mixed against the measured background colour). Nothing else in the drawing was altered.
- blink, speak1, speak2, speak3 and glance are eyes-only or mouth-only edits of listen. Codex registered each edit to listen (shift found: 0, 0 for all five) and composited only the eye or mouth region onto listen through a feathered ellipse, so everything outside that region is the listen frame itself.
- Masters were 1254 x 1254 and were scaled to 768 x 768 with premultiplied alpha; the check below runs on those 768 x 768 masters. The face box is in 768 x 768 coordinates.
Ms. Taylor frame check. Face box (x0, y0, x1, y1) = (280, 152, 482, 332). Change threshold 24/255 per channel.
