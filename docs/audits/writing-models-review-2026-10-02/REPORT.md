# Writing model answers against the lessons (2 October 2026)

Alex: "going through the lessons sometimes I have seen that model answer
essays do not match our lessons, check that please."

## How it was checked

All 63 Band 8 models (30 Task 1, 33 Task 2) were read against the eleven
Writing lessons in `src/content/lesson-bodies/` (the overview, the two
method lessons and the eight type lessons), the coach guides in
`src/data/writing-structures.ts` and the per-question plans in
`src/data/writing-plans.ts`. Five reviewers, one per group of essay types,
each turned their lessons into a list of checkable rules (with the lesson's
own words), checked every model paragraph by paragraph, and checked Task 1
figures against the chart images. Their corrections were applied in one
pass (334 replacements, each matched exactly once) and read back.

## What was wrong

Every model already had the right number of paragraphs, the right length and
the questions in the right order. The mismatches were in what the paragraphs
do, in the examiner notes, and in the plans.

Contradicting a lesson outright:
- 113 ("do the advantages of taking risks outweigh..."): no disadvantages
  paragraph at all.
- 108 (neutral "what are the advantages and disadvantages"): a verdict and a
  recommendation the question did not ask for, praised in its notes.
- 119 and 115: the conclusion introduced a new idea (115's notes said it
  did not).
- 117 and 107: the position changed between introduction and conclusion.
- 113 Task 1 (present and planned maps): each map described separately, in
  the present tense, both praised in the notes.
- Process overviews (six of seven): no stage count, and 128's notes praised
  leaving it out.
- Introductions copying the question (130, 123, 107, 117, 120, 114, 108).
- Undated charts written in the past (132, 105).
- Data slips: 120 "halved" (6% to 4%), 106 "almost all" (70%), 114 "almost
  the entire rise" (80%), 117 "still ahead", 109 "peaking" below the end.
- 95 (problem-solution, written 1 October): four effects in one paragraph
  where the lesson asks for two developed points.
- Examiner notes quoting phrases the essay does not contain (114, 115, 132,
  103) or praising what the lesson lists as a mistake (105, 125, 128).
- Plans laid out in five paragraphs where the lesson and the model use four
  (ten opinion, discussion, advantages and two-part plans), plans placing the
  concession in Body 1, and plans telling students to quote every value.

## What changed

- Models rewritten where needed, keeping Band 8 quality and the word limits
  (Task 2 270 to 316 words; Task 1 now 173 to 190, the lesson's range).
- Examiner notes and highlights rewritten wherever the text changed, so each
  describes the essay truthfully.
- Plans aligned with their lessons and models; coach guides gained the
  problem-solution and two-part points they lacked (with Russian).
- Four lesson clarifications, in English and Russian, where the lessons
  disagreed with each other or did not cover a question the bank uses:
  1. a process overview may give the number of stages (the method lesson's
     "no figures in the overview" means data figures);
  2. no dates means present simple, unless the question itself puts it in
     the past ("what graduates did");
  3. maps of a planned future: present simple for today, will / is to be +
     passive for the plan;
  4. "Is this a positive or negative development?" is an opinion question
     whose position is a verdict.
- Prompt typos: 110 ("In their advertising, businesses..."), 125 and 127
  ("at lease").
