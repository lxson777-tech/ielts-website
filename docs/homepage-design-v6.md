# Homepage design v6

The homepage presents structure as the reason IELTS feels easier. Its hero keeps the exact promise “IELTS feels EZ when you know the structure,” an inviting target-band choice, and one clear journey link. The right side uses a layered deck of four teaching cards for Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking. Each card names a real learner problem and two concise course benefits. Whole opaque cards move through one restrained shuffle, with no masked text, fading faces, pause control, or decorative CTA arrows. Visitors can also choose a card with the labelled indicators. Reduced motion shows a static card and keeps manual choice available.

The page then gives one concrete, clearly labelled AI feedback example and the verified anonymous teacher training line. It does not imply AI grading for Reading or Listening.

One raster student follows the measured road through Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking, and the selected target band. Teaching windows alternate on desktop and use one readable side on tablet and mobile. Ordinary scrolling remains native, while the road stops are optional keyboard-accessible shortcuts. The student uses the relevant study pose at each stop and an articulated distance-driven gait between them. The band remains a target throughout, and the happy arrival leads to the existing study-plan route.

Generated student pose and walk images were locally separated into transparent individual PNGs with the user's authorization. The public page contains no certificate files or personal identity details.
