# Design direction

IELTS is EZ should feel warm, intelligent, calm, and encouraging. The identity is built from warm white `#F8F7F3`, ink `#202522`, coral `#E55D48`, muted text `#69706C`, and quiet borders around `#DEDFD7`. Coral marks progress and key actions. Ink carries important buttons so they remain clear and accessible.

Plus Jakarta Sans is the compact, confident display face. Inter is the reading and interface face. Headings use strong 700 to 800 weights, close spacing, and short readable lines. Layouts use generous space, thin borders, restrained shadows, and rounded shapes with purpose.

The homepage is a simple editorial scroll story across Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking. The skill name is the dominant heading, followed by one reassuring benefit and three concise teaching points. On desktop, the panels alternate right, left, right, left while the road bends to meet a node beside each panel. Tablet and mobile use one compact side. One shared student travels the continuous road, changes pose at each stop, and may be brought to a stop by clicking its node. There are no game rules, tabs, camera movement, progress, or required choices.

Each skill panel opens once with a shallow dimensional entrance from beside the student, then settles into a flat, calm reading surface. Mobile uses a smaller two-dimensional reveal and reduced motion shows everything immediately. The effect supports the teaching story without changing native scrolling.

AI is a central product promise, shown through concrete capabilities rather than futuristic decoration. The homepage pairs the campus preview with an “AI IELTS tutor” workspace that compares a sample response, a specific annotation, and a stronger version. It accurately presents estimated writing criteria, pronunciation feedback from recorded speaking, next-step guidance, attempt history, and the live speaking examiner. Sample feedback is labeled, and the interface does not claim custom model training, official scoring, scientific validation, or guaranteed results.

The brand promise is “IELTS feels easier when you know the structure.” Supporting copy names clear methods for all four skills, guided practice, and AI feedback that shows what to improve. The tutor label is simply “Coach” until a personal name is chosen.

The final hero headline is “IELTS feels easy when you know the structure,” with “easy” and “structure” in coral. A layered Speaking Part 1 study workspace shows Answer, Reason, and Example moving into a clear structure, followed by one specific Coach improvement. The selected 6.5, 7, 7.5, or 8 band is always described as a target. The road continues beyond Speaking to that target as the final destination.

The travelling student uses the generated raster artwork and locally processed transparent cutouts, with dedicated learning poses and walk frames. Keep the single-student road contract and do not substitute simplified SVG character drawings.

Do not return to sliding raster characters, floating pose sheets, quiz missions, camera following, or required homepage interactions. The road may respond to native scroll, but must never control the viewport. This scroll story is a local demo and has passed runtime and visual QA. Study pages use the sticky 76px header and a 216px desktop workspace rail. Mobile collapses to one accessible menu. Avoid purple gradients, glowing AI imagery, fake counters, testimonials, and paid claims.
