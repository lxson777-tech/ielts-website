# IELTS is EZ homepage brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation, based on Alex's supplied answers.

1. Vibe: modern, premium, minimalist, playful, almost futuristic. Warm white, ink and coral. No purple gradient or template card wall.
2. Journey: a student struggles with all four IELTS skills, joins the platform, runs along a clear learning path, faces each skill, breaks through confusion and reaches a chosen target band.
3. Energy: overwhelmed but hopeful opening, calm clarity, playful challenge, explosive breakthrough, resolved celebration.
4. Feeling: recognition, relief, curiosity, determination, pride. The memorable moment is the student punching through the confusion wall.
5. Signature seed: the target band changes every destination label and the route at the page edge records progress through the journey.
6. Aesthetic family: playful premium minimal.
7. World: one continuous illustrated journey, expressed through varied scene devices rather than a literal video flight.
8. Assets: no supplied imagery required. Original inline vector character and CSS scenery, with the brand's existing type and colors.

## Feeling curve

- Opening: seen and encouraged, caused by the visibly overwhelmed runner beside the plain promise.
- Route: relieved, caused by scattered preparation resolving into four connected steps.
- Encounters: curious and capable, caused by small interactive coach tips and direct routes into real tools.
- Breakthrough: determined and energized, caused by the character cracking the confusion wall.
- Finish: proud and ready, caused by the selected target waiting at the finish line.

## Peak

"It is the site where the student punches straight through IELTS confusion." The peak lives between the skill encounters and finish.

## Tell-someone sentence

It is the site where an illustrated student runs through the four IELTS battles and breaks through a wall on the way to their target band.

No authored silent scroll. Every held passage changes depth, route position, lateral position, or the breakthrough state.
