# IELTS is EZ, v3 concept board

Status: proposed and unapproved visual direction.

Actual lessons, trainers, tests, and data remain unchanged and are required later. No prices or custom domain have been chosen. Incidental generated stock scene and motivational copy are not approved content.

Created with built-in image generation as one board showing three screens: homepage, dashboard, and lesson.
