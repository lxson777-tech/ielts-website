## Homepage concept

Follow a drawn, animated student who is overwhelmed by all four IELTS skills. At the beginning of the journey, the learner sets a target band so the destination feels personal. As the visitor scrolls, the story moves along one continuous path: joining the platform, training, overcoming obstacles, and reaching that target. The four encounters are Reading, Writing, Speaking, and Listening. Lessons, practice, and quiet AI help should be visible within those moments. End on readiness and a clear call to action around the learner's score goal. A target is an aim, never a guarantee.

Proposed scene rhythm:

1. Overload: the student faces all four skills.
2. Training ground: the platform gives structure through lessons and practice.
3. Four encounters: the student works through Reading, Writing, Speaking, and Listening, with AI support shown in context.
4. Readiness and CTA: the student reaches the personalized target and starts.
