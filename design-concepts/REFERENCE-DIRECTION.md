# Paid IELTS platform reference direction

## Approved platform name

The current approved brand name is **IELTS is EZ**. Existing concept images and filenames remain historical exploration assets and should not be renamed.

## Observed references

- [IELTSPractice](https://ieltspractice.io): warm white, dark ink, blue and teal accents, DM Sans, calm spacing, centered hero, glossy icons, and previews that show real exam work plus AI feedback. Borrow the product visibility and exam context. Avoid generic template rows.
- [IELTS GG / GoPrep](https://goprep.gg/): near white surfaces, bold rounded DaysOne headings, Preppy pill lettering, a restrained mascot, a huge 9.0 promise, and a tabbed showcase of eight tools. Identity is confirmed by [GoPrep terms](https://goprep.gg/terms) and the [Kazakhstan App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/kz/app/goprep-formerly-ielts-gg/id6787395636). Borrow a memorable type and brand character. Avoid letting the mascot dominate learning.
- [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org/): off white canvas, oversized serif headline, and a live interactive lesson panel in the hero. Borrow real lesson visibility: show one IELTS task working step by step, with quiet AI guidance beside it. Avoid copying the child focused STEM illustration language. Visual inspected.
- [Speak](https://www.speak.com/): spacious white layout, soft blue controls, and a clear Learn, Practice, Apply rhythm. Borrow a visible study loop so learners always know what stage they are in. Avoid faint low contrast headings and excessive letter spacing. Visual inspected.
- [Readwise Reader](https://readwise.io/read): dark navy to violet gradient, minimal navigation, and product screenshots used as editorial proof. Borrow a focused reading surface with highlighting, notes, and in context AI explanations. Avoid making the full IELTS product dark or turning the homepage into a feature inventory. Visual inspected.

## Proposed direction

Use soft white and ink with one accent color to be chosen. Pair a distinctive friendly display face with an exceptionally readable text face. Replace the metal ribbon with a real interactive IELTS hero. Make AI useful and quiet inside the task, not a separate chatbot spectacle. Keep Reading calm and document like. Retain all current content while expressing it through fewer, clearer moments of product proof.
