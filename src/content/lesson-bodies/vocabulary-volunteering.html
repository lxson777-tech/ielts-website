Topic Vocabulary

Volunteering & Community

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
volunteera person who works without pay to help others"Volunteers run the library on Saturdays."
charityan organisation that raises money to help people in need"The charity provides meals for homeless people."
donationmoney or goods given to help a person or cause"A small monthly donation is worth more than one large gift."
fundraisingcollecting money for a cause"The school held a fundraising event for the local hospital."
community centrea building where local people meet for activities"The community centre offers free classes for pensioners."
non-profit organisationan organisation that exists to do good rather than make money"The shelter is run by a non-profit organisation."
local residentsthe people who live in a particular area"Local residents organised the clean-up themselves."
good causean aim that is morally worthwhile"They ran the marathon in aid of a good cause."
support networkthe people and services that help someone in difficulty"A strong support network helps new parents enormously."
civic dutya responsibility that comes with being a member of a society"Many people see voting as a civic duty."

Key Collocations

  • volunteer at / for an organisation
  • lend a hand
  • raise money for charity
  • get involved in a project
  • reach out to people in need
  • rely on volunteers

Useful Essay Phrases

  • "Volunteering benefits the volunteer almost as much as the people being helped."
  • "Services that depend entirely on volunteers are fragile, and governments should not rely on them."
  • "Requiring students to volunteer risks turning generosity into another obligation."
  • "A few hours of someone's time is often worth more than a donation."
  • "Strong communities are built by people who know their neighbours."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. Demand at the local ________ rises every winter as heating bills climb. (food bank)
  2. He runs free classes in the evening to ________. (give back to the community)
  3. The campaign started as a ________ movement organised by a handful of neighbours. (grassroots)
  4. Regular contact with others reduces ________ among elderly people. (social isolation)
  5. The shelter could not open its doors without its ________. (volunteers)
  6. A well-designed campaign can ________ of a problem most people ignore. (raise awareness)
  7. Helping at the centre gave me a ________ when I first moved here. (sense of belonging)
  8. Many employers value ________ gained through unpaid work. (work experience)
  9. Most people see voting as a basic ________. (civic duty)
  10. The ________ relies on small monthly gifts rather than a few large ones. (charity)
