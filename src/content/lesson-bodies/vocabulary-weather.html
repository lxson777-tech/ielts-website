Topic Vocabulary

Weather, Seasons & Nature

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
climatethe usual weather conditions of a region over a long period"The country has a dry climate with very little rainfall."
humidhaving a lot of moisture in the air"Summers here are hot and extremely humid."
mildnot very cold and not very hot"The winters are surprisingly mild for this latitude."
droughta long period with little or no rain"The drought destroyed most of the harvest."
floodingwater covering land that is usually dry"Heavy rain caused flooding across the region."
heatwavea period of unusually hot weather"The heatwave lasted for three weeks."
forecasta statement of what the weather is expected to be"The forecast says it will clear up by the afternoon."
seasonalhappening at, or suited to, a particular time of year"Seasonal work in agriculture attracts thousands of migrants."
countrysideland outside towns and cities, used for farming or left natural"We escape to the countryside whenever we can."
coastlinethe land along the edge of the sea"The country has a coastline of over two thousand kilometres."

Key Collocations

  • brighten up / clear up

  • bask in the sunshine

  • get some fresh air

Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "Autumn is my favourite season, mainly because of the colours."
  • "The weather has a bigger effect on my mood than I would like to admit."
  • "Summers are getting noticeably hotter than when I was a child."
  • "On a nice day everyone heads outside, so the parks are packed."
  • "We have four proper seasons, which I think I would miss if I moved away."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. After two months without rain, the ________ ruined the wheat crop. (drought)
  2. A three-week ________ pushed temperatures above forty degrees. (heatwave)
  3. We were caught in a sudden ________ and arrived completely soaked. (downpour)
  4. The air is hot and ________, which makes sleeping difficult. (humid)
  5. It took me weeks to ________ to the altitude and the cold. (acclimatise)
  6. Short, dark winters can cause ________ in some people. (seasonal affective disorder)
  7. Earthquakes and floods are the ________ the region fears most. (natural disasters)
  8. We took the longer, more ________ road along the coast. (scenic)
  9. Winters here are ________ rather than freezing, which surprises visitors. (mild)
  10. The warm climate encourages an ________ all year round. (outdoor lifestyle)
