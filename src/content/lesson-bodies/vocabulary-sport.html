Topic Vocabulary

Sport & Fitness

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
physical fitnessthe condition of being strong and healthy as a result of exercise"Physical fitness declines quickly when people stop exercising."
team sporta sport, such as football or basketball, played between groups of players"Team sports teach children how to cooperate."
competitive sportsport in which the main aim is to win against others"Competitive sport is not suitable for every child."
spectatora person who watches an event, especially a sports match"Over sixty thousand spectators filled the stadium."
sportsmanshipfair and respectful behaviour towards opponents, whether you win or lose"Shaking hands after a match is a sign of good sportsmanship."
professional athletea person who is paid to compete in a sport"Professional athletes train for several hours every day."
sports facilitiesplaces such as pitches, pools and gyms where people can do sport"Public sports facilities should be affordable for everyone."
regular exercisephysical activity that is done often, for example several times a week"Regular exercise lowers the risk of heart disease."
staminathe physical or mental strength to keep going for a long time"Long-distance running builds stamina."
international sporting eventa large competition, such as the Olympic Games, involving many countries"Hosting an international sporting event can transform a city's image."

Key Collocations

  • take up a sport
  • keep / stay fit
  • set / break a record
  • promote an active lifestyle
  • host a major sporting event
  • work out at the gym

Useful Essay Phrases

  • "Team sports teach children cooperation and how to deal with defeat."
  • "The salaries of top athletes reflect the vast commercial value of modern sport."
  • "Hosting a major event can leave a city with expensive stadiums that are rarely used."
  • "Public money would do more good at grassroots level than at the elite level."
  • "Sporting success can unite a country in a way that few other things can."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. Athletes caught using ________ are usually banned from competition for several years. (performance-enhancing drugs)
  2. Congratulating the winner, even after a painful defeat, shows real ________. (sportsmanship)
  3. Investing in ________ gives every child the chance to discover a talent. (grassroots sport)
  4. The ________ hoped that the Games would attract tourists for years afterwards. (host nation)
  5. Doctors recommend ________ of at least thirty minutes on most days of the week. (regular exercise)
  6. Without ________ from large companies, many smaller sports could not fund their competitions. (sponsorship)
  7. Swimming long distances requires ________ rather than speed. (stamina)
  8. Cutting ________ from the school timetable may harm children's health. (physical education)
  9. Cheap public ________ such as swimming pools make exercise possible for low-income families. (sports facilities)
  10. An Olympic gold medal can produce a surge of ________ across a whole country. (national pride)
