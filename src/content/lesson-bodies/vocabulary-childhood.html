Topic Vocabulary

Childhood & Growing Up

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
grow upto develop from a child into an adult"I grew up in a small town by the sea."
memorysomething you remember from the past"My earliest memory is of my grandmother's kitchen."
nostalgiaa fond feeling about the past"Old photographs fill me with nostalgia."
toddlera very young child who has just learnt to walk"The park has a play area for toddlers."
primary schoolthe first stage of formal education, for young children"I still remember my primary school teacher's name."
playgroundan outdoor area where children play"Half of my childhood was spent on that playground."
imaginaryexisting only in the mind"Like many children, I had an imaginary friend."
disciplinetraining to obey rules, and the punishment used to enforce them"Discipline at my school was strict but fair."
spoilto give a child everything they want, harming their character"Grandparents are allowed to spoil their grandchildren."
formative yearsthe early period of life when character is shaped"The formative years matter more than any later stage of education."

only childa child with no brothers or sisters"As an only child I spent a lot of time reading."

siblinga brother or sister"Having older siblings taught me to argue my case."

milestonean important stage in someone's development"A first day at school is a milestone for parents as well."
pocket moneya small amount of money given regularly to a child"Pocket money teaches children to save before they earn."
unstructured playplay that children organise themselves, without adult rules"Unstructured play teaches negotiation and imagination."
independencethe ability to do things without help from others"Small chores give children a sense of independence."
bring back memoriesto make you remember something from the past"That song always brings back memories of school."

Key Collocations

  • look back on your childhood
  • grow out of a habit
  • get up to mischief
  • be brought up by someone
  • have fond memories of
  • learn the hard way

Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "Looking back, I realise how much freedom we had."
  • "One memory that really stands out is a trip we took to the mountains."
  • "Children today have far more opportunities but far less unsupervised time."
  • "I was brought up mainly by my grandparents, which I am grateful for now."
  • "It was a simple childhood, but I would not change it."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. Those early years, the ________, shape character more than anything later. (formative years)
  2. We spent the evening ________ about our school days. (reminiscing)
  3. I had a rather ________ childhood, so leaving home was difficult at first. (sheltered)
  4. Managing their own ________ teaches children to save before they ever earn. (pocket money)
  5. ________, where children invent the rules themselves, teaches negotiation. (Unstructured play)
  6. Walking to school alone gave me a real sense of ________. (independence)
  7. That song always ________ of my grandmother's house. (brings back memories)
  8. Summers then felt endless and completely ________. (carefree)
  9. A child's first day at school is an emotional ________ for the whole family. (milestone)
  10. Old photographs fill me with ________ for a time I barely remember. (nostalgia)
