Topic Vocabulary

Leisure & Entertainment

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
leisure timethe time when you are not working or studying and can do what you enjoy"I spend most of my leisure time outdoors."
pastimean activity that you do regularly for enjoyment"Gardening is a popular pastime among retired people."
unwindto relax after a period of work or stress"I listen to music to unwind after work."
genrea particular type of film, book or music"My favourite film genre is science fiction."
blockbustera very successful film, usually one made with a huge budget"The summer blockbuster broke box-office records."
streaming servicean online platform for watching films and series or listening to music"Streaming services have changed the way people watch television."
live musicmusic performed in front of an audience rather than recorded"The café has live music every Friday evening."
bestsellera book that sells in very large numbers"Her first novel became an international bestseller."
binge-watchto watch many episodes of a series one after another"We binge-watched the whole series in one weekend."
outdoor activitiesthings people do outside for fun, such as hiking, cycling or camping"The region is ideal for outdoor activities such as hiking."

Key Collocations

  • take up / give up a hobby
  • be keen on / be into something
  • while away the time
  • have a night in
  • catch a film
  • get hooked on a series

Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "What I enjoy most about it is that it takes my mind off work."
  • "I am not a huge fan of horror films, to be honest."
  • "It is a great way to unwind after a long day."
  • "I got into photography a couple of years ago and I have never looked back."
  • "If I had more free time, I would love to take up a musical instrument."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. After a stressful week, a long walk by the river helps me to ________. (unwind)
  2. The novel was such a ________ that I finished it in a single evening. (page-turner)
  3. Instead of waiting a week for each episode, many viewers now ________ an entire series. (binge-watch)
  4. Cinemas are struggling because so many people now subscribe to a ________. (streaming service)
  5. The melody is good, but I honestly cannot understand the ________. (lyrics)
  6. I decided to ________ yoga last year, and it has really improved my sleep. (take up)
  7. Crime fiction is the ________ that I read most often. (genre)
  8. The film had a ________ at the end that nobody in the cinema expected. (plot twist)
  9. A few days by the sea are all I need to ________. (recharge my batteries)
  10. Reading fantasy novels is pure ________ for me, a break from everyday worries. (escapism)
