Topic Vocabulary

Success, Goals & Ambition

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
achievementsomething important that you succeed in doing"Finishing the degree while working was my biggest achievement."
goalsomething you aim to do or become"Setting a clear goal makes progress much easier to measure."
determinationthe refusal to give up despite difficulty"It took real determination to finish the course."
motivationthe reason that makes you want to do something"Motivation matters more than talent over a long project."
failurea lack of success"Failure taught me more than any of my successes."
opportunitya chance to do something you want"The scholarship gave me an opportunity I would never have had."
potentialthe ability to develop and succeed in the future"Good teachers see potential where others see problems."
hard worksustained effort"Nothing here happened by luck; it was hard work."
rewardsomething good you get in return for effort"The reward for two years of study was a job I actually enjoy."
confidencebelief in your own ability"Small early successes build confidence."

Go Further

Ten more words for the same topic. Learn these once the table above feels comfortable.

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
perseverancecontinuing to try despite difficulty or delay"Learning a language is largely a matter of perseverance."
setbacka problem that delays progress"Every project meets setbacks; what matters is the response."
turning pointa moment after which a situation changes completely"That job was the turning point in my career."
self-fulfilmentthe feeling of having developed your abilities fully"For many people, self-fulfilment matters more than a high salary."
recognitionpublic acknowledgement of what someone has done"Recognition from colleagues means more than a bonus."
ambitious targetan aim that is difficult but not impossible"The company set an ambitious target for the year."
out of your comfort zonein a situation that feels unfamiliar or difficult"You only improve when you step out of your comfort zone."
long-termcovering or planned for a long period of time"Long-term goals need short-term steps to stay believable."
measure of successthe standard by which success is judged"Money is only one measure of success, and a poor one."
trade-offa situation in which you accept less of one thing to get more of another"Every ambitious plan involves a trade-off with your free time."

Key Collocations

  • set / achieve a goal
  • work towards something
  • overcome an obstacle
  • pay off (of effort)
  • live up to expectations
  • give up on a dream

Useful Speaking & Essay Phrases

  • "The achievement I am proudest of is not the largest one."
  • "Success should be measured by more than income alone."
  • "Failure is a far better teacher than easy success."
  • "Ambition is healthy as long as it does not consume everything else."
  • "Setting a goal is easy; the difficult part is the daily routine that reaches it."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. Learning an instrument is mostly a matter of ________ rather than talent. (perseverance)
  2. Every long project meets a ________ at some point. (setback)
  3. You improve fastest when you step ________. (out of your comfort zone)
  4. For many people, ________ matters more than a large salary. (self-fulfilment)
  5. Income is one ________, but a very incomplete one. (measure of success)
  6. That first contract was the ________ in her whole career. (turning point)
  7. Years of early mornings finally ________ when she qualified. (paid off)
  8. Every ambitious plan involves a ________ with your free time. (trade-off)
  9. A teacher who sees a child's ________ can change that child's life. (potential)
  10. Quiet ________ from colleagues often means more than a bonus. (recognition)
