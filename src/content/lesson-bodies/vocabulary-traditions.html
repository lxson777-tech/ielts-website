Topic Vocabulary

Traditions, Festivals & Customs

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
customa way of behaving that is usual in a particular society"Removing your shoes indoors is a custom in many countries."
rituala set of actions always performed in the same way on a particular occasion"The ceremony follows a ritual that has not changed for centuries."
ancestorsthe people in your family who lived a long time before you"The festival honours the memory of our ancestors."
generationall the people of roughly the same age within a family or society"The recipe has been passed down through four generations."
national holidaya day when most people in a country do not work, marking something important"Independence Day is a national holiday celebrated across the country."
ceremonya formal event held to mark an important occasion"The wedding ceremony took place in the village church."
folk musictraditional music of a particular people or region"Folk music is being revived by younger musicians."
traditional dressthe clothing that people of a country have worn historically"Guests wore traditional dress to the celebration."
superstitiona belief that certain things bring good or bad luck"The superstition about the number thirteen is surprisingly widespread."
hospitalityfriendly and generous treatment of guests"The region is famous for the hospitality of its people."

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
pass downto give knowledge or a custom to younger people"These songs were passed down orally rather than written."
die outto disappear completely, over time"Many local customs are dying out as young people move to the cities."
reviveto bring something back after it has almost disappeared"The town has revived a festival that stopped fifty years ago."
heritage sitea place protected because of its historical or cultural importance"The old city was declared a heritage site in 1997."
intangible heritagetraditions, skills and stories rather than buildings or objects"Cooking methods and songs are part of a country's intangible heritage."
cultural erosionthe gradual weakening of a culture, usually under outside influence"Cultural erosion is one of the least discussed effects of globalisation."
communalshared by, or done together with, all members of a group"The meal is communal, with everyone eating from the same dishes."
rite of passagean event that marks an important stage in a person's life"Graduation is a rite of passage in many cultures."
time-honouredrespected because it has existed for a long time"They prepare the dish using a time-honoured method."
commercialisedchanged so as to make money, often losing its original meaning"Critics complain that the festival has become too commercialised."

Key Collocations

  • observe / uphold a tradition
  • hand down a custom to the next generation
  • celebrate a festival
  • preserve cultural heritage
  • fall into decline
  • take pride in your roots

Useful Essay Phrases

  • "Traditions give people a sense of belonging that modern life often fails to provide."
  • "Once a custom is lost, it is almost impossible to recover it in its original form."
  • "Globalisation has brought variety to daily life, but at the cost of local distinctiveness."
  • "Schools are the most practical place to keep traditional skills alive."
  • "A festival that exists only to attract tourists has already lost much of its meaning."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. Songs and recipes are examples of ________ rather than monuments or buildings. (intangible heritage)
  2. Local dialects and crafts are ________ as younger people leave for the cities. (dying out)
  3. Graduation and marriage are both a ________ that marks a new stage of life. (rite of passage)
  4. The village has managed to ________ a harvest festival that stopped decades ago. (revive)
  5. Many argue that the event has become too ________ to mean anything to local people. (commercialised)
  6. Recipes are usually ________ from parents to children rather than written in books. (passed down)
  7. The old quarter became a protected ________ in the 1990s. (heritage site)
  8. Guests are welcomed warmly; the region is known for its ________. (hospitality)
  9. The belief that a broken mirror brings bad luck is a common ________. (superstition)
  10. ________ is one of the quieter costs of globalisation, and it happens slowly. (Cultural erosion)
