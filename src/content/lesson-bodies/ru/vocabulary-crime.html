juvenile delinquencyпреступное поведение среди несовершеннолетних"Rising juvenile delinquency has prompted calls for earlier intervention programmes."
deterrentто, что удерживает человека от совершения поступка, особенно через страх последствий"Harsher sentencing is often proposed as a deterrent to violent crime."
rehabilitationпроцесс, который помогает преступнику вернуться к законопослушной жизни"Prisons that prioritise rehabilitation tend to have lower reoffending rates."
recidivismсклонность осуждённого преступника совершать повторные преступления"High recidivism rates suggest that short prison sentences do little to change behaviour."
white-collar crimeненасильственное преступление ради финансовой выгоды, обычно совершаемое профессионалами"White-collar crime such as fraud often goes undetected for years."
law enforcementгосударственные органы и должностные лица, отвечающие за соблюдение закона"Increased funding for law enforcement has not always led to lower crime rates."
capital punishmentузаконенная казнь человека как наказание за преступление"Public opinion on capital punishment remains sharply divided."
custodial sentenceнаказание, связанное с лишением свободы"Judges are increasingly favouring community service over short custodial sentences."
socioeconomic factorsсоциальные и экономические условия, например бедность, которые влияют на поведение"Many criminologists argue that socioeconomic factors are the root cause of crime."
petty crimeмелкие правонарушения, например кражи в магазинах или вандализм"Petty crime tends to rise in areas with high youth unemployment."

Показать упражнение

Заполните пропуски словами из этого раздела:

  1. Many criminologists believe that ________ such as poverty and unemployment are the root cause of crime. (socioeconomic factors)
  2. Prisons that focus on ________ rather than pure punishment tend to see lower reoffending rates. (rehabilitation)
  3. Community programmes have been shown to reduce ________ among first-time young offenders. (juvenile delinquency)
  4. ________ such as fraud and embezzlement can cause enormous financial harm despite being non-violent. (White-collar crime)
  5. High rates of ________ suggest that many prisons fail to prepare inmates for life after release. (recidivism)
  6. Instead of prison, minor offenders may be ordered to do ________ such as cleaning public parks. (community service)
  7. The risk of a ________ is one of the strongest arguments against the death penalty. (miscarriage of justice)
  8. As more of life moves online, ________ such as identity theft has grown rapidly. (cybercrime)
  9. Youth clubs and better lighting are inexpensive forms of ________. (crime prevention)
  10. A young ________ who learns a trade in prison is less likely to return to crime. (offender)
