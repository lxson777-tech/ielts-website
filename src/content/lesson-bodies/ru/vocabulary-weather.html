Погода, времена года и природа

Слово или фразаЗначениеПример в контексте
climateпривычные погодные условия региона на протяжении долгого времени"The country has a dry climate with very little rainfall."
humidс большим содержанием влаги в воздухе"Summers here are hot and extremely humid."
mildне слишком холодный и не слишком жаркий"The winters are surprisingly mild for this latitude."
droughtдолгий период почти без дождей"The drought destroyed most of the harvest."
floodingвода, покрывающая обычно сухую землю"Heavy rain caused flooding across the region."
heatwaveпериод необычно жаркой погоды"The heatwave lasted for three weeks."
forecastсообщение о том, какая ожидается погода"The forecast says it will clear up by the afternoon."
seasonalпроисходящий в определённое время года или подходящий для него"Seasonal work in agriculture attracts thousands of migrants."
countrysideземля за пределами городов, используемая для сельского хозяйства или оставленная нетронутой"We escape to the countryside whenever we can."
coastlineполоса земли вдоль моря"The country has a coastline of over two thousand kilometres."

  • "The weather has a bigger effect on my mood than I would like to admit."
  • "Summers are getting noticeably hotter than when I was a child."
  • "On a nice day everyone heads outside, so the parks are packed."
  • "We have four proper seasons, which I think I would miss if I moved away."
Показать упражнение

Заполните пропуски словами из этого раздела:

  1. After two months without rain, the ________ ruined the wheat crop. (drought)
  2. A three-week ________ pushed temperatures above forty degrees. (heatwave)
  3. We were caught in a sudden ________ and arrived completely soaked. (downpour)
  4. The air is hot and ________, which makes sleeping difficult. (humid)
  5. It took me weeks to ________ to the altitude and the cold. (acclimatise)
  6. Short, dark winters can cause ________ in some people. (seasonal affective disorder)
  7. Earthquakes and floods are the ________ the region fears most. (natural disasters)
  8. We took the longer, more ________ road along the coast. (scenic)
  9. Winters here are ________ rather than freezing, which surprises visitors. (mild)
  10. The warm climate encourages an ________ all year round. (outdoor lifestyle)
