Родной город и описание мест

Слово или фразаЗначениеПример в контексте
bustlingполный оживлённой деятельности"The market is bustling from early in the morning."
picturesqueпривлекательный, как на картине"It is a picturesque village surrounded by hills."
run-downв плохом состоянии из-за отсутствия ухода"The area near the station is rather run-down."
city centreглавная центральная часть города"The city centre is closed to traffic at weekends."
outskirtsокраины города, вдали от центра"We live on the outskirts, about twenty minutes from the centre."
landmarkхорошо известное здание или объект, который узнают люди"The tower is the city's most famous landmark."
sceneryприродные особенности местности, воспринимаемые как вид"The scenery along the coast is spectacular."
populationчисло людей, живущих в каком-либо месте"The population has doubled in twenty years."
lively atmosphereощущение энергии и активности в каком-либо месте"The old quarter has a lively atmosphere in the evenings."
peacefulтихий и спокойный"It is a peaceful place to raise children."

Ключевые коллокации

  • be located / be situated in
  • grow up in a place
  • be famous for something
  • get away from the hustle and bustle
  • a stone's throw from
  • settle down in a place

Полезные фразы для Speaking

  • "I grew up in a small town about an hour from the capital."
  • "What I like most about it is that everything is within walking distance."
  • "It is not particularly beautiful, but it has real character."
  • "The area has changed enormously since I was a child."
  • "If I had the choice, I would settle down somewhere quieter."
Показать упражнение

Заполните пропуски словами из этого раздела:

  1. The district near the old factories is ________ and badly needs investment. (run-down)
  2. We live on the ________, which is quieter but further from work. (outskirts)
  3. The town is ________: the castle dates from the twelfth century. (steeped in history)
  4. It is a truly ________ city, where dozens of languages are spoken daily. (cosmopolitan)
  5. The bridge is the most photographed ________ in the country. (landmark)
  6. The best cafés are ________, in the streets tourists never reach. (off the beaten path)
  7. Smaller cities often offer a better ________ for the same salary. (quality of life)
  8. The main shopping street was ________, so cars are no longer allowed. (pedestrianised)
  9. The town is ________, with a fast train to the capital every half hour. (well-connected)
  10. We are lucky: the sea is right on our ________. (doorstep)
