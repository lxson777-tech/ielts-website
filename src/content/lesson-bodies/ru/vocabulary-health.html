Тематическая лексика

Здоровье и благополучие

Слово или фразаЗначениеПример в контексте
obesityзаболевание, связанное с избыточной массой тела, которое угрожает здоровью"Childhood obesity rates have doubled in many countries over the past 20 years."
sedentary lifestyleобраз жизни с малой физической активностью"An increasingly sedentary lifestyle is contributing to a range of chronic illnesses."
mental healthпсихологическое и эмоциональное благополучие"Awareness of mental health issues has grown significantly in recent years."
preventable diseaseболезнь, которую можно было предотвратить с помощью образа жизни или медицинского вмешательства"A large proportion of deaths in developed nations are caused by preventable diseases."
public healthздоровье населения в целом, которым занимается государство"Governments have a responsibility to invest in public health education."
healthcare systemорганизованная система больниц, клиник и специалистов, оказывающих медицинскую помощь"Underfunded healthcare systems struggle to cope with ageing populations."
nutritionпроцесс получения пищи, необходимой для здоровья и роста"Poor nutrition in childhood can have lasting consequences for development."
epidemic / pandemicширокое распространение болезни в регионе / по всему миру"The COVID-19 pandemic exposed deep inequalities in global healthcare."
wellbeingсостояние комфорта, здоровья и счастья"Employers are increasingly focusing on the wellbeing of their workforce."
life expectancyсреднее число лет, которое, как ожидается, проживёт человек"Advances in medicine have dramatically increased life expectancy worldwide."

Ключевые коллокации

  • tackle obesity / mental health issues
  • promote a healthy lifestyle / wellbeing
  • strain the healthcare system
  • chronic illness / disease / stress
  • deteriorating mental / physical health
  • allocate funding to healthcare / prevention

Полезные фразы для эссе

  • "The rising incidence of obesity is placing enormous strain on…"
  • "Prevention is far more cost-effective than treatment."
  • "Mental health has historically been underfunded relative to physical health."
  • "Governments have a duty to create environments that promote healthy lifestyles."
  • "The long-term consequences of poor nutrition are well-documented."
Показать упражнение

Выберите правильное слово, чтобы дополнить каждое предложение:

  1. A increasingly ________ (sedentary / preventable) lifestyle means fewer people are doing regular exercise. (sedentary)
  2. Many experts argue that ________ (prevention / epidemic) campaigns are more effective than treating conditions after they develop. (prevention)
  3. Poor diet and lack of exercise are the leading causes of ________ (obesity / wellbeing) in children. (obesity)
  4. Workers who feel supported in their ________ (nutrition / wellbeing) are significantly more productive. (wellbeing)
  5. The ageing population will inevitably ________ (strain / promote) public healthcare systems in coming decades. (strain)
