Matching Headings

What is it?

The official name for this task is Matching Headings. You are given a list of headings (roman numerals: i, ii, iii…) and a passage with labelled paragraphs (A, B, C…). You must choose the best heading for each paragraph. There are always more headings than paragraphs or sections: some headings won't be used.

Important Distinction

One or more paragraphs may already be matched with a heading as an example printed on the question paper. That heading is then used up and cannot be the answer to any of the numbered questions, so cross it off before you start.

A heading is a title: it must describe the whole paragraph's main idea, not just one detail inside it. One matching detail ≠ the correct heading.

How to Approach It

  1. Read through all the headings first and note synonyms/paraphrases.
  2. For each paragraph, read to find its central aim: what is the paragraph mainly about?
  3. Distinguish between the main idea and supporting examples or details.
  4. Eliminate headings that only match one sentence in the paragraph.
  5. Watch for headings that look similar. Compare them carefully.
  6. Your answer must be a roman numeral, written the way it appears in the list (e.g. iii).
⚠ Trap: choosing a heading that matches one detail, not the whole paragraph ⚠ Trap: headings with similar wording. Look at meaning, not just words
