Topic Vocabulary

Music, Film & Television

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
soundtrackthe music used in a film or programme"The soundtrack is more famous than the film itself."
directorthe person who controls how a film is made"The director spent four years on a single project."
plotthe series of events that make up a story"The plot was predictable but the acting was superb."
charactera person in a film, book or play"The main character is a detective who has lost his memory."
subtitlesthe translation or transcript shown at the bottom of the screen"Younger viewers are increasingly happy to watch films with subtitles."
box officethe money a film makes from ticket sales"The sequel disappointed at the box office."
sequela film or book that continues the story of an earlier one"Studios prefer sequels because the audience already exists."
reviewa written opinion about a film, book or album"The film received mixed reviews."
classical musicformal European music written for orchestras and similar ensembles"He fell in love with classical music through film scores."
live concerta musical performance in front of an audience"A live concert has an energy that no recording captures."

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
cinematographythe art of filming and lighting a film"The cinematography alone makes the film worth watching."
special effectsimages or sounds created artificially for a film"Modern special effects can make anything look real."
low-budgetmade with very little money"It was a low-budget film that became an unexpected hit."
critically pannedstrongly criticised by reviewers"The remake was critically panned but made a fortune."
typecastalways given the same kind of role"After that series she was typecast as the villain."
cultural exportpart of a country's culture that becomes popular abroad"Korean television has become the country's best-known cultural export."
dubbingreplacing the original speech in a film with another language"Some countries prefer dubbing, others subtitles."
streaming platforman online service that shows films and series on demand"Streaming platforms now fund more drama than the studios do."
background musicmusic played quietly while something else is happening"I need background music in order to concentrate."
universal appealthe quality of being liked by people everywhere"Music has a universal appeal that crosses every language barrier."

Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. The film was ________ by reviewers, yet audiences loved it. (critically panned)
  2. Foreign films are shown with ________ so the original voices can still be heard. (subtitles)
  3. Korean drama has become the country's most successful ________. (cultural export)
  4. The ________ is stunning, especially the scenes shot at sunrise. (cinematography)
  5. Because the first film sold so well, a ________ was announced within a month. (sequel)
  6. Despite huge advertising, the film failed at the ________. (box office)
  7. A ________ has an atmosphere that a recording can never reproduce. (live concert)
  8. Since that role, he has been ________ as the comic sidekick. (typecast)
  9. Most new drama is now funded by a ________ rather than by a television channel. (streaming platform)
  10. The ________ was so memorable that it outlived the film. (soundtrack)
