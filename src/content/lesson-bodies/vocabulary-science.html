Topic Vocabulary

Science & Space

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
scientific researchcareful study carried out to discover new facts about the world"Scientific research into new antibiotics is urgently needed."
hypothesisan idea that explains something but has not yet been proved"The experiment was designed to test the hypothesis."
experimenta controlled test carried out to find out whether an idea is true"The experiment was repeated in three different laboratories."
evidencefacts or signs that show whether something is true"There is strong evidence that the drug is effective."
findingsthe information discovered as the result of a study"The findings were published in a leading journal."
discoverythe act of finding or learning something that was not known before"The discovery of penicillin transformed medicine."
theorya set of ideas, supported by evidence, that explains how something works"The theory of evolution is supported by a vast body of evidence."
space explorationthe investigation of space using spacecraft and telescopes"Space exploration has produced technologies that we now use every day."
satellitea machine placed in orbit around the Earth to collect or send information"Weather forecasts rely on data from satellites."
research fundingmoney provided to pay for scientific studies"Research funding is increasingly tied to commercial results."

genetic engineeringdeliberately changing the genes of a living thing"Genetic engineering could eliminate some inherited diseases."
empirical datainformation gathered by observation or experiment rather than theory"Policy should be based on empirical data, not on opinion."
STEM subjectsscience, technology, engineering and mathematics"Governments want more girls to study STEM subjects."
space debrisbroken satellites and other rubbish left in orbit around the Earth"Space debris poses a growing danger to working satellites."
crewed missiona space flight that carries astronauts"A crewed mission to Mars would take many months each way."
scientific literacya basic understanding of how science works and what its results mean"Scientific literacy helps citizens to judge health claims for themselves."
ethical concernsworries about whether an action is morally right"Cloning raises serious ethical concerns."
commercial spaceflightspace travel run by private companies for profit"Commercial spaceflight has sharply reduced the cost of launching satellites."

Key Collocations

  • conduct / carry out research
  • test a hypothesis
  • fund scientific research
  • make a discovery
  • raise ethical concerns
  • push back the frontiers of knowledge

Useful Essay Phrases

  • "Opponents argue that the money spent on space programmes would be better used to tackle problems on Earth."
  • "Many everyday technologies began as by-products of space research."
  • "Scientific progress tends to move faster than the laws designed to regulate it."
  • "Governments, rather than private companies, should fund research that serves the public interest."
  • "Without public trust in science, even the best research has little effect on behaviour."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. Before a new medicine can be sold, it must pass a ________ involving thousands of volunteers. (clinical trial)
  2. A scientist begins with a ________ and then designs an experiment to test it. (hypothesis)
  3. Thousands of pieces of ________ now orbit the Earth and threaten working satellites. (space debris)
  4. ________ means that other experts examine a study before it is published. (Peer review)
  5. Critics of ________ argue that the money should be spent on poverty and disease instead. (space exploration)
  6. Decisions about public health should rest on ________ rather than on rumour. (empirical data)
  7. Changing the genes of crops through ________ could help to feed a growing population. (genetic engineering)
  8. Universities complain that ________ now depends on promising quick commercial results. (research funding)
  9. The study's ________ suggest that sleep has a direct effect on memory. (findings)
  10. Encouraging girls to choose ________ would help to fill the shortage of engineers. (STEM subjects)
