Topic Vocabulary

Animals & Wildlife

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
wildlifeanimals, birds and plants that live in natural conditions"The wetlands are home to a rich variety of wildlife."
endangered speciesa type of animal or plant that may soon disappear completely"The giant panda is no longer classed as an endangered species."
extinctionthe situation in which a type of animal or plant no longer exists"Many species face extinction within the next fifty years."
natural habitatthe place where an animal or plant normally lives and grows"Animals behave very differently in their natural habitat."
habitat lossthe destruction of the places where animals live"Habitat loss is the main threat to orangutans."
captivitythe state of being kept in a zoo or cage rather than living free"Some animals breed poorly in captivity."
animal welfarethe health, comfort and proper treatment of animals"Animal welfare laws set minimum standards for farms."
poachingthe illegal hunting or catching of wild animals"Poaching has almost wiped out the black rhino."
livestockfarm animals such as cattle, sheep and pigs"Raising livestock uses far more land than growing crops."
wildlife reservea protected area where wild animals can live safely"The wildlife reserve employs local people as rangers."

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
animal testingthe use of animals in experiments for medicines or cosmetics"Animal testing for cosmetics is banned in many countries."
animal rightsthe idea that animals deserve to be protected from cruelty and exploitation"Animal rights groups campaign against keeping whales in tanks."
breeding programmean organised effort to increase the numbers of a rare species"A breeding programme saved the species from disappearing."
illegal wildlife tradethe unlawful buying and selling of wild animals or parts of them"The illegal wildlife trade is worth billions of dollars a year."
overfishingcatching so many fish that their numbers cannot recover"Overfishing has caused cod stocks to collapse."
food chainthe series of living things in which each one feeds on the one below it"Plastic in the ocean eventually enters the human food chain."
invasive speciesa plant or animal brought into an area where it harms the native wildlife"Invasive species often have no natural predators in their new home."
rewildingreturning land to its natural state and bringing back the animals that once lived there"Rewilding projects have brought beavers back to British rivers."
marine lifethe animals and plants that live in the sea"Oil spills are devastating for marine life."
animal crueltybehaviour that causes unnecessary suffering to animals"Penalties for animal cruelty have been increased."

Key Collocations

  • protect / safeguard endangered species
  • be driven to extinction
  • keep animals in captivity
  • release animals into the wild
  • crack down on poaching
  • restore natural habitats

Useful Essay Phrases

  • "Zoos play a valuable role in conservation and in educating the public."
  • "Opponents maintain that wild animals suffer when they are kept in captivity."
  • "Protecting natural habitats is more effective than breeding animals in zoos."
  • "Testing on animals remains necessary for some medical research, although alternatives are being developed."
  • "The loss of a single species can disturb an entire ecosystem."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. Rangers patrol the park at night to prevent ________ of elephants for their ivory. (poaching)
  2. ________ caused by farming and logging is the greatest threat to most wild animals. (Habitat loss)
  3. Thanks to a successful ________ in zoos, the species has been returned to the wild. (breeding programme)
  4. ________ has reduced tuna numbers so sharply that some fisheries have closed. (Overfishing)
  5. Critics argue that large mammals should never be kept in ________. (captivity)
  6. An ________ such as the grey squirrel can push native animals out of their territory. (invasive species)
  7. Many people believe ________ is acceptable for medicines but not for cosmetics. (animal testing)
  8. Without urgent action, hundreds of species face ________ this century. (extinction)
  9. Through ________, wolves and beavers have been returned to areas where they once lived. (rewilding)
  10. Plastic waste in the oceans is a serious danger to ________. (marine life)
