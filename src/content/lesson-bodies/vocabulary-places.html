Topic Vocabulary

Hometown & Describing Places

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
bustlingfull of busy activity"The market is bustling from early in the morning."
picturesqueattractive in the way a picture would be"It is a picturesque village surrounded by hills."
run-downin bad condition through lack of care"The area near the station is rather run-down."
city centrethe main central area of a city"The city centre is closed to traffic at weekends."
outskirtsthe outer parts of a town, away from the centre"We live on the outskirts, about twenty minutes from the centre."
landmarka well-known building or feature that people recognise"The tower is the city's most famous landmark."
scenerythe natural features of an area, seen as a view"The scenery along the coast is spectacular."
populationthe number of people living in a place"The population has doubled in twenty years."
lively atmospherea feeling of energy and activity in a place"The old quarter has a lively atmosphere in the evenings."
peacefulquiet and calm"It is a peaceful place to raise children."

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context

steeped in historyhaving a long and important history"The town is steeped in history, with buildings from the twelfth century."
cosmopolitancontaining people and influences from many countries"It is a cosmopolitan city where you hear ten languages on one street."
deprived areaa district where many people are poor and services are weak"Investment is badly needed in the most deprived areas."
pedestrianisedchanged so that only people on foot may use it"The main street was pedestrianised ten years ago."
on the doorstepvery close to where you live"We have the mountains right on our doorstep."
sprawlingspreading over a large area in an untidy way"It is a sprawling city with no clear centre."
well-connectedeasy to reach by road, rail or air"The town is well-connected, with trains every ten minutes."
local identitythe particular character that makes a place itself"Chain stores have weakened the local identity of many high streets."
quality of lifehow comfortable, healthy and happy life is in a place"Smaller cities often offer a better quality of life."

Key Collocations

  • be located / be situated in
  • grow up in a place
  • be famous for something
  • get away from the hustle and bustle
  • a stone's throw from
  • settle down in a place

Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "I grew up in a small town about an hour from the capital."
  • "What I like most about it is that everything is within walking distance."
  • "It is not particularly beautiful, but it has real character."
  • "The area has changed enormously since I was a child."
  • "If I had the choice, I would settle down somewhere quieter."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. The district near the old factories is ________ and badly needs investment. (run-down)
  2. We live on the ________, which is quieter but further from work. (outskirts)
  3. The town is ________: the castle dates from the twelfth century. (steeped in history)
  4. It is a truly ________ city, where dozens of languages are spoken daily. (cosmopolitan)
  5. The bridge is the most photographed ________ in the country. (landmark)
  6. The best cafés are ________, in the streets tourists never reach. (off the beaten path)
  7. Smaller cities often offer a better ________ for the same salary. (quality of life)
  8. The main shopping street was ________, so cars are no longer allowed. (pedestrianised)
  9. The town is ________, with a fast train to the capital every half hour. (well-connected)
  10. We are lucky: the sea is right on our ________. (doorstep)
