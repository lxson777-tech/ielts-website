Topic Vocabulary

Ageing & Retirement

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
retirementthe period of life after you stop working"People are spending far longer in retirement than they used to."
retirement agethe age at which people are expected to stop working"Several governments have raised the retirement age."
the elderlyold people considered as a group"The elderly were hit hardest by the rise in heating costs."
senior citizena polite term for an older person, usually over sixty-five"Senior citizens travel free on public transport here."
care homea place where old people live and are looked after"Care homes are expensive and often understaffed."
dependantspeople who rely on someone else financially"Workers now support more dependants than a generation ago."
longevitylong life"Improved longevity is a success that creates new problems."
generational dividethe gap in attitudes between older and younger people"Housing costs have widened the generational divide."
experienceknowledge gained over years of doing something"Older workers bring experience that cannot be trained quickly."
savings for old agemoney set aside to live on after retiring"Few young workers are putting aside savings for old age."

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
demographic shifta lasting change in the make-up of a population"The demographic shift towards older populations is irreversible."
working populationthe people of working age who support everyone else"A shrinking working population must fund a growing number of pensions."
age discriminationtreating someone unfairly because of their age"Age discrimination makes it hard for over-fifties to change jobs."
intergenerationalinvolving people from different age groups"Intergenerational projects bring schools and care homes together."
dementiaa condition affecting memory and thinking, common in old age"Dementia will affect one in three people over eighty."
mobilitythe ability to move around easily"Reduced mobility isolates older people more than illness does."
active ageingstaying physically and socially involved in later life"Active ageing keeps people healthier for longer."
phased retirementgradually reducing working hours instead of stopping suddenly"Phased retirement suits both employers and older staff."
burden on the statea cost that the government has to carry"Politicians too often describe older people as a burden on the state."
life stagea distinct period of a person's life"Retirement is now a life stage that can last thirty years."

Key Collocations

  • grow old / age gracefully
  • retire from work
  • care for an elderly relative
  • draw a pension
  • stay active in later life
  • place a strain on public services

Useful Essay Phrases

  • "A longer life is an achievement, not a problem, but it does have to be paid for."
  • "Older employees offer experience and reliability that younger staff cannot yet match."
  • "Raising the retirement age is unfair to people in physically demanding jobs."
  • "Keeping older people socially involved is cheaper than treating the illnesses of isolation."
  • "Describing an ageing population purely as a burden ignores everything those people contribute."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. The ________ towards older populations cannot now be reversed. (demographic shift)
  2. A shrinking ________ has to fund a growing number of pensions. (working population)
  3. ________ makes it far harder for people over fifty to change jobs. (Age discrimination)
  4. ________ projects bring schoolchildren and care-home residents together. (Intergenerational)
  5. Reduced ________ cuts older people off from friends and shops. (mobility)
  6. ________ allows staff to reduce their hours gradually rather than stop overnight. (Phased retirement)
  7. Programmes that encourage ________ keep people healthier for longer. (active ageing)
  8. Several governments have raised the ________ to sixty-seven. (retirement age)
  9. Politicians should stop describing older citizens as a ________. (burden on the state)
  10. Improved ________ means retirement can now last thirty years. (longevity)
