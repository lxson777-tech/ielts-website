Key Collocations

  • get on well with someone

Useful Speaking Phrases

  • "The person who has influenced me most is probably my grandmother."
  • "What I admire about him is that he never talks about his own achievements."
  • "She has a way of making everyone feel comfortable."
  • "He is the kind of person you can call at two in the morning."
  • "I would describe myself as fairly easy-going, although I can be stubborn about work."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. Despite becoming famous, he has remained completely ________. (down to earth)
  2. Working from home suits people who are ________ enough to manage their own time. (self-disciplined)
  3. I have always ________ my older sister, who raised three children alone. (looked up to)
  4. Everyone says I ________ my mother: we even laugh the same way. (take after)
  5. A ________ teacher can make students care about a subject they once hated. (charismatic)
  6. She rarely gives advice, but she is ________, which is often what people need. (a good listener)
  7. Children are surprisingly ________ and recover from setbacks faster than adults. (resilient)
  8. He is ________: he will never make a noise when the baby is asleep. (considerate)
  9. Travelling alone made me far more ________ about other ways of living. (open-minded)
  10. If he promises to help, he will be there. He is utterly ________. (reliable)
