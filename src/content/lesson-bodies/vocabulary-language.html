Topic Vocabulary

Language & Communication

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
mother tonguethe first language a person learns as a child"Children learn best when they are first taught in their mother tongue."
bilingualable to speak two languages well"Bilingual employees are in high demand."
fluencythe ability to speak a language smoothly and easily"Living abroad is the fastest route to fluency."
language barrierdifficulty communicating because people do not share a language"The language barrier made it hard to explain the problem to the doctor."
lingua francaa language used between people who do not share a first language"English has become the lingua franca of international business."
dialecta form of a language that is spoken in one particular region"The local dialect contains words that outsiders cannot understand."
accenta way of pronouncing words that shows where a person comes from"She speaks English with a slight French accent."
body languagecommunication through posture, gestures and facial expressions"Body language often reveals more than words do."
foreign languagea language that is not spoken in your own country"Learning a foreign language opens the door to another culture."
translationchanging speech or writing from one language into another"Machine translation is improving but still makes errors."

Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. English serves as a ________ at international conferences, where few people share a first language. (lingua franca)
  2. With fewer than a hundred speakers left, it is classed as an ________. (endangered language)
  3. Children who grow up with two languages at home usually become ________. (bilingual)
  4. Living with a local family provides total ________ in the language. (immersion)
  5. Migrants who face a ________ may struggle to find work or see a doctor. (language barrier)
  6. Gestures and eye contact are forms of ________ that differ between cultures. (non-verbal communication)
  7. Most university courses taught in English require proof of ________. (proficiency)
  8. A simple ________ over the delivery date cost the company an important client. (miscommunication)
  9. As more people switch to global languages, ________ is declining. (linguistic diversity)
  10. People are usually most comfortable expressing emotion in their ________. (mother tongue)
