Sentence Completion

What is it?

The official name for this task is Sentence Completion. You are given incomplete sentences with a gap (___). You must find the correct word(s) from the passage to complete each sentence. You must use exact words from the passage and stay within the word limit printed with the task. The limit changes from task to task, for example "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER" or "ONE WORD ONLY", so read it every time.

Key Rules

  • Words must come directly from the passage
  • Obey the word limit strictly. 4 words when the limit is 3 = wrong
  • A hyphenated word such as "check-in" counts as one word, and a number counts as one word
  • Contracted words such as "they're" are never tested, so you will never need one
  • Spelling must be correct
  • Answers follow the order of the passage

How to Approach It

  1. Read each incomplete sentence and identify keywords before searching.
  2. Think about what type of word is missing (noun, verb, adjective, number?).
  3. Scan the passage using keywords and synonyms to locate the relevant section.
  4. Read carefully around that section and identify the exact word(s) that complete the sentence logically and grammatically.
  5. Write the answer. Check spelling and word count.
⚠ Trap: paraphrasing instead of copying exact words ⚠ Trap: going over the word limit ⚠ Trap: ignoring grammar. The completed sentence must make grammatical sense
