Fashion & Clothing

Word / PhraseMeaningExample in Context
fashion trenda style of clothing that is popular at a particular time"Fashion trends now change several times a season."
casual wearcomfortable, informal clothing"Most offices have moved towards casual wear."
formal dresssmart clothing worn for serious or official occasions"The invitation asked for formal dress."
designer labelclothing made by a famous fashion company"People pay far more for a designer label than for the fabric."
second-handpreviously owned by someone else"Second-hand clothing is no longer considered embarrassing."
wardrobeall the clothes a person owns"I try to keep my wardrobe small and practical."
comfortablepleasant to wear"I choose comfortable clothes over fashionable ones."
uniforma set of clothes worn by everyone in a school or organisation"School uniform removes visible differences in income."
stylethe particular way a person dresses"Her style is simple and never changes much."
fabricthe cloth that clothes are made from"Natural fabrics are more expensive but last longer."

Key Collocations

  • be in / go out of fashion
  • dress up / dress down
  • follow the latest trends
  • suit someone
  • throw away clothes after a season
  • mend / repair clothing

Useful Speaking & Essay Phrases

  • "I would not say I follow fashion, but I like to look presentable."
  • "Fast fashion is cheap for the buyer and expensive for the planet."
  • "School uniform hides the differences in what families can afford."
  • "Clothing is one of the simplest ways people express who they are."
  • "Buying fewer, better-made garments is both cheaper and greener in the long run."
Show Practice Exercise

Fill in the gaps using words from this section:

  1. ________ produces cheap clothes quickly, and most are thrown away within a year. (Fast fashion)
  2. Millions of tonnes of ________ end up in landfill every year. (textile waste)
  3. Campaigners accuse the brand of relying on ________ labour abroad. (sweatshop)
  4. A plain wool coat is a ________ purchase that never looks dated. (timeless)
  5. For teenagers, clothing is an important form of ________. (self-expression)
  6. School ________ makes differences in family income far less visible. (uniform)
  7. Buying ________ clothing costs more at first but far less over ten years. (durable)
  8. The restaurant has a strict ________ and will turn away trainers. (dress code)
  9. ________ shops have become fashionable among people who care about waste. (Second-hand)
  10. Natural ________ such as cotton and wool last longer than synthetic ones. (fabrics)
